For researchers, lecturers and students.

How it helps

Judge a source at a glance

Colour-coded Q1 to Q4 badges appear beside each journal in your search results, so you can screen a long list quickly.

A discreet tab on article pages

A slim edge tab shows the quartile and SJR. Click it for the full card and a link to the journal's Scimago page.

Private by design

The Scimago data is bundled and looked up on your device. No account, no tracking, no network requests.

Install

Chrome Web Store listing: coming soon. Until then, install from source:

  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Search on Scopus, ScienceDirect or Google Scholar. Nothing to configure.

Data and privacy

Journal metrics come from the Scimago Journal & Country Rank (based on Scopus data) and are a guide, not a verdict. Journals Scimago does not list show no badge. ARTi SciPeek makes no network requests and collects no data; its only stored data is the edge tab's position and collapsed state in your browser's local storage. Read the full privacy policy.
