See a journal's Quartile, SJR, H-index and SINTA rank right where you read, with no extra tab.

ARTi SciPeek adds a colour-coded badge beside journal names on Scopus, ScienceDirect and Google Scholar, and a slim tab on 20 publisher sites and on Indonesian university journal sites. Built for researchers, lecturers and students. Fully offline, no data collected.

It shows Scimago journal metrics (journal ranking): Quartile (Q1 to Q4), SJR and H-index, with one-click links to the journal's Scimago and Scopus pages. For Indonesian journals it also shows the SINTA accreditation (S1 to S6, the national ranking) with Impact and H5-index, and a link to the journal's SINTA profile.

Useful for:
• Researchers choosing where to read, cite or submit
• Lecturers and reviewers checking the standing of a source
• Indonesian authors checking a journal's SINTA level next to its international ranking
• Students judging whether a paper comes from a reputable journal
• Anyone screening a long list of search results quickly

WHERE IT WORKS
Search results: a small colour-coded badge appears beside each journal name, including on Google Scholar profile pages.

Article and journal pages: a slim tab sits on the right edge of the page. It shows the quartile and SJR at a glance; click it for the full card with SJR, H-index, the SINTA block and "Scimago", "Scopus" and "SINTA" buttons. It works on 20 major publisher sites and on journal and article pages of Open Journal Systems (OJS) sites on Indonesian university domains (*.ac.id). The full list is in the GitHub README. You can drag the tab up or down.

Colours at a glance: Q1 green, Q2 amber, Q3 orange, Q4 red. SINTA is shown in navy, because it is a different scale: SINTA 1 is the best national level and is not the same as Q1.

HOW TO USE
1. Install the extension. There is nothing to set up and no sign-in.
2. Search as usual, or open an article or journal page on a supported site.
3. Read the badge or click the edge tab. Use the Scimago, Scopus or SINTA button for the full journal profile (Scopus may ask you to sign in).

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
The Scimago and SINTA data are bundled inside the extension and looked up on your own device. SciPeek makes no network requests, needs no account, and collects no data. Nothing you browse is sent anywhere. On university sites it acts only on journal and article pages, not on the rest of the site.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Metrics come from a Scimago Journal & Country Rank snapshot and a snapshot of the public SINTA journal list taken in October 2026. SINTA levels change with each accreditation period, so the data ages. Journals that neither source lists (for example, very new ones) show no badge.
• Matching uses the journal's ID or ISSN when the page provides it, otherwise the exact journal title. A SINTA match for a journal that Scimago already identified is made by ISSN only.
• Metrics are a guide, not a verdict. Use them alongside your own judgement of a paper and its field.
