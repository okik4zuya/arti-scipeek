See a journal's Quartile, SJR, H-index and SINTA rank right where you read, with no extra tab.

ARTi SciPeek adds a colour-coded badge beside journal names in search results, and a slim tab on article and journal pages. It is built for researchers, lecturers and students, works fully offline, and collects no data.

WHAT YOU SEE
• Quartile (Q1 to Q4), SJR and H-index from Scimago, with one-click links to the journal's Scimago and Scopus pages.
• For Indonesian journals: the SINTA level (S1 to S6, the national accreditation), Impact and H5-index, with a link to the SINTA profile.

WHO IT HELPS
Researchers choosing where to read, cite or submit. Lecturers and reviewers checking a source. Indonesian authors comparing a SINTA level with the international ranking. Students judging where a paper was published.

WHERE IT WORKS
Search results on Scopus, ScienceDirect and Google Scholar get a small badge beside each journal name. Article and journal pages get a slim tab on the right edge of the page: it shows the quartile and SJR, and a click opens the full card. It works on 20 major publisher sites and on journals hosted on Indonesian university domains (*.ac.id) that run Open Journal Systems. The full list is in the GitHub README.

Colours: Q1 green, Q2 amber, Q3 orange, Q4 red. The national level is navy because it is a different scale: level 1 is the best national level and is not the same as Q1.

HOW TO USE
1. Install. There is nothing to set up and no sign-in.
2. Search as usual, or open an article or journal page.
3. Read the badge, or click the edge tab and use its buttons for the full profile (Scopus may ask you to sign in).

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
The ranking data is bundled inside the extension and looked up on your own device. The extension makes no network requests, needs no account, and collects no data. On university sites it acts only on journal and article pages.

GOOD TO KNOW
• The data comes from a Scimago snapshot and a snapshot of the public SINTA list taken in October 2026. National levels change with each accreditation period, so the data ages. A journal that neither source lists shows no badge.
• Matching uses the journal's ID or ISSN when the page provides it, otherwise the exact title.
• Metrics are a guide, not a verdict.

BAHASA INDONESIA
Menampilkan kuartil, SJR, H-index, dan peringkat nasional (S1–S6) di samping nama jurnal. Data tersimpan di dalam ekstensi, bekerja offline, tanpa akun, dan tidak mengumpulkan data. Peringkat nasional bukan padanan kuartil.
